Privacy Policy — SITE DIM

Last updated: September 2026

No data collection

SITE DIM does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on a server. Every measurement, survey, and calculation performed in the app stays only on your device.

Camera and AR sensors

The app uses your iPhone's camera and sensors (ARKit, LiDAR on compatible models) to perform augmented-reality surveys. These images are never recorded, transmitted, or shared with anyone — they're processed in real time, directly on your device, to place measurement points.

Photo Library

If you choose to save an image or export to your photo library, the app accesses it write-only, with your explicit permission. We never have access to the rest of your photo library.

Data export

When you export a plan view (image or .dxf) or the summary table (PDF), the file is generated locally on your device. Sharing it (AirDrop, email, Files, etc.) happens through iOS's standard share menu, entirely under your control. We never have access to a copy of that file.

No tracking, no advertising

SITE DIM includes no analytics, advertising tracking, or third-party data sharing tools.

Contact
