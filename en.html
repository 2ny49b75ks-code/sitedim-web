SITE DIM

Measure. Calculate. Build.

SITE DIM turns your iPhone into a construction takeoff tool. Aim, tap, and the app traces the outline in augmented reality, closes the shape, and calculates area, perimeter and volume automatically — no more tape measure, no more manual math on site.

Download on the App Store

Six survey types, one tool

Every surface type has its own capture flow, built around how it's actually measured on site.

🧱

Walls

Trace a wall's outline and subtract doors and windows directly — net area is calculated automatically.

⬇️

Floors

Same tracing and net-area calculation, applied to floor surveys.

⬆️

Ceilings

Aim upward — the app adapts automatically to the surface's orientation.

🔺

Roofs

Measure slope and façade separately, across multiple segments if a panel is interrupted (a dormer, etc.) — area = total slope × total façade, never an auto-calculated pitch.

📏

Linear

Trim, baseboards, and any point-to-point length — a direct, single measurement.

⛰️

Terrain

Full topographic survey with triangulation, excavation volume calculation, and up to 3 sequential fill layers — each with its own volume.

Capture fast, capture right

A shape selector at the top of the screen picks the fastest capture method for each surface.

  • Rectangle — 2 measurements (width, length), automatic right angles.
  • Circle — a single diameter measurement.
  • Half-moon — a chord plus one radius point.
  • Full trace — for any irregular shape, a polygon with variable angles.
  • Corner squaring — a blue button snaps an angle near 90°/180° to its exact value, without ever moving an already-confirmed point.
  • Automatic point validation — a captured point turns from red to green on its own, ready for the next one.
  • Dual dimension labels — metric above, feet-inches-fraction below every line, just like a physical tape measure.

Precision built for the job site

📡

LiDAR preference

On compatible devices, every captured point favors a hit on the LiDAR mesh over an estimated plane, for higher accuracy.

🎯

Averaged, locked position

Each point is computed once from several samples, snapped to the real surface, then never moves again — no drift over the course of a survey.

🔋

Built to stay cool

AR rendering automatically slows down whenever the aim is still, keeping your device cool during a long survey.

Your surveys, ready to share

Every captured surface shows a calibrated plan view — pinch to zoom, drag to pan — identical on screen and in every export.

Image export (PNG) Vector export (.dxf) Full-page PDF table

The full survey summary — every surface, area, perimeter and volume — exports as a clean, properly laid-out PDF table, ready to print or send to a client or supplier. The .dxf file opens natively in CAD software (AutoCAD and equivalents).
