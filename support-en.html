Support — SITE DIM

SITE DIM is an augmented-reality quantity takeoff app for construction: walls, floors, ceilings, roofs, trim, and terrain surveys with excavation volume calculation.

Frequently asked questions

The app isn't detecting surfaces

Make sure you're in a well-lit environment and move your device slowly to give the camera time to analyze the surface before aiming at a point.

Does SITE DIM require a LiDAR iPhone?

Yes — SITE DIM requires an iPhone or iPad equipped with a LiDAR sensor (iPhone 12 Pro/13 Pro/14 Pro/15 Pro/16 Pro and later, or an equivalent iPad Pro) to perform augmented-reality surveys.

How do I export my survey?

From a surface's detail screen, export a calibrated plan view as an image (PNG) or as a vector drawing (.dxf, AutoCAD-compatible). From the survey summary, tap the share icon at the top of the screen to export the full table (areas, perimeters, volumes) as a full-page, print-ready PDF.

Can I measure a rectangular or round shape faster?

Yes — a shape selector at the top of the capture screen (wall, floor, ceiling, roof, terrain) lets you pick Rectangle, Circle, or Half-moon for a 1-or-2-tap measurement, in addition to the usual full trace.
